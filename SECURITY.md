# Security

Launchpad runs AI bots on your Mac with access to your files and accounts, so security reports are welcome and taken
seriously.

## Reporting a problem

Please **don't open a public issue** for a security problem.

Use GitHub's private reporting: on this repository, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it lets someone do. Use dummy values, never real tokens or other
people's data.

You'll get a reply as soon as the maintainer can. A fix is released before details are made public.

## What counts

Anything that lets text from outside (a web page, a file, a message, a selected text) or a bot get further than it should:
past the approval cards, the sandbox, the protected files, the guard, or the update path; or that exposes a secret.

## Supported versions

Only the latest build from `main`.
